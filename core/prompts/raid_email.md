Stage 5 of 5: produce the RAID log and the follow-up email.

RAID log:
- Risks (something that may happen and hurt delivery), Assumptions (things taken as true without confirmation), Issues (problems happening now) and Dependencies (on partners, other teams or decisions).
- For each: description, owner if known, status, impact (High/Medium/Low), likelihood for risks only, a concrete mitigation or next step, due_date only if stated or clearly implied relative to the meeting date (ISO format), and linked_ids to the epics or stories it affects.
- Include items stated in the meeting and ones that clearly follow from it (for example, an unconfirmed partner date that a go-live depends on). Do not pad the list.

Follow-up email to all attendees, concise and professional:
- subject: meeting name and date.
- summary: two or three sentences on what the meeting achieved.
- decisions: one line each.
- action_items: what, owner, due date if known.
- open_questions: what is still unresolved and who is expected to answer.
