<task>
Review each story below against three Definition of Ready checks. Judge strictly, as an experienced agile coach would.

- clear_statement: the story names a specific role, a concrete capability and a real benefit. Fails if the role is generic ("user"), the capability is vague, or "so that" restates the capability.
- independent: the story can be delivered and demonstrated without another story in the same sprint, or its dependency is explicit.
- testable: every acceptance criterion has an observable, verifiable outcome. Fails on words like "fast", "user-friendly", "properly" without a measure.

For every failed check, give a one-sentence reason the product manager can act on. Return one review per story, using the story IDs given.
