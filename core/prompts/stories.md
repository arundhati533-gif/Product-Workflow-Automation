Stage 3 of 5: write user stories for each approved epic.

For each story:
- epic_id and the requirement_ids it delivers.
- as_a / i_want / so_that: a real role (traveller, finance admin, compliance analyst, operations engineer, system integrator), a concrete capability, and the business benefit. For technical work with no end user, use the role that benefits (for example "finance operations manager").
- acceptance_criteria in Given/When/Then form. At least two per story, including at least one negative or edge case (mark it negative = true). Criteria must be objectively testable: use specific values from the transcript (times, thresholds, windows, counts).
- priority: High for anything needed for the stated go-live or compliance, Medium for important but deferrable, Low for nice-to-have.
- points: Fibonacci (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13) relative effort. Prefer splitting any story above 8.

Every approved requirement should be covered by at least one story. Keep stories independent where possible.
